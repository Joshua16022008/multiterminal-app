# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short summary: be respectful, accept constructive feedback and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Contact the repository maintainers privately to report conduct concerns; do not publish personal information in issues.
